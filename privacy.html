Application data practices

Privacy notice

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This notice describes the current personal-use desktop application NovaTales Lab and this informational website. The application is operated on its channel operator’s Windows computer.

Google data accessed

When the operator grants Google OAuth consent, the application may access:

  • The connected YouTube channel’s identifier, title, public statistics and list of uploaded videos.
  • Authorized YouTube Analytics reports, such as views, engaged views, watch time, viewing duration, likes, comments and subscriber changes, where these reports are available.
  • The ability to upload a selected video and its metadata and apply a thumbnail to the connected channel, when the operator grants the upload permission and approves the precise file and metadata.

The desktop OAuth permissions used for these features are YouTube read-only access, YouTube Analytics read-only access and YouTube upload access. Google handles sign-in and consent. NovaTales Lab does not ask the operator to provide a Google account password to the application.

Purpose and limits of use

Google data is used to identify the operator’s channel, display channel information, review its performance, and carry out an explicitly approved private video upload. The application does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized AI models. Its Google-data use is limited to the application features described in this notice.

For the current workflow, the final approval refers to a particular file, thumbnail, metadata, version and channel. Granting an OAuth permission alone does not approve a particular video upload. Videos uploaded by this workflow are created as private videos.

Storage on the operator’s computer

Production records, local analytics records, metadata, approval receipts and generated artifacts are stored in the operator’s local application database and files. The application keeps imported source footage and its checksums so that it can check integrity and avoid unintended repeated processing.

Google OAuth tokens are stored in Windows Credential Manager. The desktop OAuth client configuration is stored locally and excluded from the application’s source repository and synchronized video inbox. OAuth tokens are not included in application backups. A restored workflow requires operator reactivation before it can resume private uploads.

Data sent to other services

  • Google and YouTube: OAuth requests, authorized API queries, and an approved video file, thumbnail and metadata are sent to Google’s services to provide the connected-channel features.
  • Telegram: When configured by the operator, briefs, prompts, reference frames, previews, proposed thumbnails and metadata, operational notices and approval requests are sent through the operator’s bot to the configured private chat. Google OAuth credentials are not sent through that chat.
  • Cloud synchronization: The operator may place media in a folder synchronized by a service such as MEGA. Synchronization is performed by that service’s software under the operator’s settings. NovaTales Lab works with the locally available files.
  • Manually selected video-generation services: The operator may separately provide prompts or media to such a service. Those manual interactions are governed by the chosen service’s own practices.

The local story-generation model runs on the operator’s computer. Google OAuth tokens are not supplied to that model or to video-generation providers.

Retention, revocation and deletion

Local production files and records remain under the operator’s control until the operator removes them. Revoking the app’s Google access stops future authorized access when the revocation takes effect; it does not by itself erase previously stored local files or database records. The operator can remove the app’s stored credentials in Windows Credential Manager and separately remove local records, source files, artifacts and backups.

Google access can be reviewed or revoked from the Google account’s third-party connections settings. Copies already uploaded to YouTube, sent to Telegram or synchronized through a cloud service must be managed through the relevant service and account controls.

This informational website

These static pages do not include application sign-in, account forms, advertising scripts or site analytics added by NovaTales Lab. The hosting provider may process standard network requests, technical logs or platform cookies under its own practices. The website does not receive the operator’s application database, media files or Google OAuth tokens.

Contact and changes

For support, a privacy question or a request concerning application-held data, use the support email displayed on the Google consent screen for NovaTales Lab. This notice will be updated if the application’s data practices change.

This notice describes NovaTales Lab’s current features and data handling. Google, YouTube, Telegram, hosting and synchronization services maintain their own terms and privacy notices.
